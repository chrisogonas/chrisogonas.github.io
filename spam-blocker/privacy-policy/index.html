QwikSens Call Shield Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

QwikSens Call Shield ("the app", formerly "Spam Blocker") is developed by Chrisogonas Odhiambo. This policy explains what information the app handles, how, and the choices you have. Questions: chrisogonas@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Everything the app does happens on your phone.
  • The app has no internet permission. It cannot send your data anywhere, and it contains no ads, analytics or tracking.
  • There is no account to create.
  • We do not collect, sell or share your information. The developer never receives it.

Information the app handles

To screen calls, Android gives the app the phone number of each incoming call from a number that is not in your contacts. The app uses it to decide whether to block the call, and stores the following only on your device:

DataWhyHow long it is kept
Blocked-call history: phone number, time, and why it was blocked So you can review what was blockedUntil you clear History or uninstall the app
Recent call activity: phone number, time, and whether it was blocked To detect repeat callers and spam wavesDeleted automatically after 30 days
Your rules: number patterns, notes, and whether each is on, paused or trusted To block or allow future callsUntil you delete them or uninstall the app
Your settings: Smart protection options and quiet hours To remember your preferencesUntil you uninstall the app

The app does not read your contacts, call log, text messages, location, microphone or files. Calls from your contacts never reach the app, because Android doesn't screen them.

Permissions

  • Caller ID & spam app (call-screening role): lets Android ask the app whether to allow or block incoming calls. You grant this in a system prompt and can remove it at any time in Settings → Apps → Default apps.
  • Notifications: to tell you when a number is auto-blocked. You can turn these off in the app or in Android settings.

When information leaves your phone (only when you choose)

  • Backups you create: Back up rules saves a file containing your rules and settings to a location you pick, such as Downloads or Google Drive. It does not include call history.
  • Reporting to the FTC: Report to FTC copies the number and opens the FTC's official website in your browser. Nothing is sent unless you fill in and submit the FTC's form yourself. Your use of that site is covered by the FTC's own privacy policy.
  • Sending feedback: Send feedback opens your email app addressed to the developer. Only what you choose to send is shared.

Android backups

  • Google cloud backup: if you have it turned on, Android may back up the app's settings to your Google account. Phone numbers, call history and rules are excluded from cloud backup.
  • Phone-to-phone transfer: when you set up a new phone by connecting it directly to your old one, Android may copy the app's rules, history and settings straight to the new phone.

Deleting your information

  • History: History tab → Clear.
  • Rules: delete them in the Rules tab.
  • Recent call activity: deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Everything: uninstalling the app permanently removes all of its data from your phone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly handle information about them beyond the incoming phone numbers described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
